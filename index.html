 		Together with its newly acquired autonomous status, ABES Engineering College (ABESEC) stands as one of the premier private technical institutions in the Delhi-NCR region. Founded in 2000 and located on a 15.77-acre Wi-Fi-enabled campus along NH-09 in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, the college has grown to educate over 8,000 students supported by a network of more than 24,000 alumni. Approved by the AICTE, ABESEC holds a NAAC accreditation and has its primary undergraduate programs accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA). It consistently ranks within the 201–300 band in the NIRF engineering rankings.
